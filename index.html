Move anything in 3D with one motor

Why geoalg?

Matrices are a calculator. Motors are a supercomputer.

◎

One Motor = Rotate + Translate

Traditional libs need multiple matrices. geoalg combines rotation and translation into a single 8-component motor.

🤖

Robotics Ready

Forward kinematics, joint limits, TCP tools, trajectories, collision detection.

🎮

Game Engine Compatible

Unity (C#), Unreal (C++), Bevy (Rust), WebGL (WASM). Drop-in bindings.

⌂

Smooth Interpolation

Screw theory interpolation. No gimbal lock, no singularities.

⚙

Calculus Built-in

Derivatives, gradients, divergence, curl, Stokes theorem, dual numbers.

🌐

WebAssembly Ready

Runs in any browser at 22KB. No plugins, no install.
